# Slack Update Template

Use this format for quick team updates in Slack channels.

---

## Format

**2-3 lines max, scannable, casual tone**

[Opening line with context - what's happening]

[Key insight or decision - the meat]

[Action item or next step - what's needed]

---

## Style Guidelines

- ✅ Use emojis naturally (🚀 ✅ 📊 etc.)
- ✅ Keep it conversational
- ✅ Link to docs for details
- ✅ @mention people for visibility
- ✅ Break up text with line breaks

## Example

Quick update on dark mode 🌙

Just wrapped user testing - 9/10 enterprise customers said this is a blocker for their team adoption. Engineering estimates 2 sprint implementation.

Next: @Thomas for prioritization call Thursday

---

**When to use:** Quick updates to engineering team, design sync, weekly progress posts
